生息光語

Language of Living Light
閃閃發光設計工作室　戴綺芬 設計
台北　165 m²

Located in the heart of urban Taipei, this 165-square-meter healthy, eco-conscious residence embodies a sustainable design philosophy expressed through the language of light, transforming a fragmented floor plan into a living composition of light, shadow, and breath.

The spatial layout is reconfigured around a central daylight axis, allowing natural light to guide movement throughout the home. Bamboo-wood slatted panels seamlessly integrate five interior doors into a continuous architectural surface, creating visual clarity and cohesion. Mirror walls and softly illuminated display cabinets dissolve the sense of a narrow corridor, extending the visual connection between the living and dining areas and the lush green balcony beyond.

Custom storage is carefully recessed into the walls, preserving a clean and uncluttered interior. Under gentle side lighting, the refined texture of ultra-thin stone veneers quietly reveals its natural beauty, adding depth and warmth to the minimalist palette.

Low-impact materials, layered greenery, and carefully controlled natural light work together to create a balanced indoor environment that enhances comfort throughout the seasons.

The bedroom incorporates negative ion technology, transforming the space into a tranquil sanctuary that supports physical and mental well-being. Designed as a restorative retreat, it fosters a calm atmosphere where people, materials, and the surrounding environment exist in quiet harmony.
